# Data and credentials

The application prepares the factual `state` and question instructions sent to the selected provider. Review that payload against your application's data policy before enabling requests. Keep keys in your existing secret store or process environment and keep local acceptance thresholds and expected answers with the application.

Persist attempt identity and pending state before dispatch. Preserve known charges on failures, retain uncertain attempts for reconciliation, and reuse matching completed outcomes through your runner. Review redirects, timeouts and response limits when supplying an injected transport.

Use synthetic or anonymized inputs in public issues. Keep credentials, real conversations, private data, raw provider responses and operational receipts in the owning application's private storage. If a report includes sensitive material, prepare a sanitized reproducer before opening a public issue.
